## Description

Screenshots multiplying like rabbits?
Random PDFs having a party on your desktop?
Downloads spilling across your screen?

RackOff swoops in, sorts your desktop clutter into tidy dated folders, and gets out of your way. One click. Desktop's clean. You're back to work.

✨ ONE-CLICK INSTANT RELIEF
• Click the sparkles in your menu bar
• All matching loose files are swept clean in under one second
• Instant visual peace and focus for your workspace

🗂️ THREE WAYS TO ORGANIZE
• Quick Archive: Everything swept into clean daily dated folders (YYYY-MM-DD)
• Sort by Type: Screenshots go to Screenshots, PDFs to Documents, media to Media
• Smart Clean: You decide which file types get organized and where they land

🎯 TARGET WHAT BUGS YOU
• Screenshots and screen recordings from debugging sessions
• PDFs and document downloads
• Media and audio files
• Loose archives and downloaded installers
• Leaves project folders untouched if you choose

🛡️ 100% SAFE & REVERSIBLE
• Nothing is ever deleted — only relocated
• Everything sits safely in your local ~/Documents/Archive/ folder
• Open your archive folder with one click directly from the app

🔒 100% PRIVATE • ZERO SUBSCRIPTIONS
• No accounts. No tracking. No ads. Zero network requests.
• Runs completely locally on your Mac with Apple sandbox security.
• $9.99 once. Yours forever. No monthly or annual fees.

Clean desktop. Clear head.
